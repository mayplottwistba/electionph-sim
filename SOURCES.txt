PROVINCE POPULATION BASELINE
The province population numbers currently embedded in game.js are 2020 Census of Population and Housing counts. They are population baselines, not registered-voter counts. The current list contains 82 Philippine provinces. Province totals do not include all highly urbanized cities that are statistically separate from their surrounding provinces, nor does the 82-province list include NCR as a province.

GAME MODEL NOTES
- Direct province campaign: PHP 20,000 per province; one direct province campaign per candidate-player per province per week.
- Independent random events are not caused by opponents. They may include positive/negative word of mouth, typhoon/flooding, earthquake, ashfall, storm surge, transport disruption, power interruption, cost-of-living concerns, and local community events.
- Calamities apply a simulated turnout disruption to a share of the province's population. They do not change the official census population and do not imply that affected residents died.
- Sector populations overlap. Do not sum sector estimates to derive a unique number of people.
- All campaign outcomes, voter shifts, turnout impacts, and forecasts are game simulations, not polling, predictions, or real election data.
